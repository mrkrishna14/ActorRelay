  • Each system watches only its own piece.
  • The lesson from the last incident sits in a log nobody reads at change time.

The product

Negotiate. Remember. Refuse.

  • Negotiate: every setpoint change is a proposal, and every connected machine's agent votes.
  • Remember: each agent cites past runs, then is scored against what really happened.
  • Refuse: one no blocks the change with evidence. A safer target follows, and a human gets a 10-second veto.
  • Across plants: a rule from Plant A is cited in Plant B.

Why both tools

Actors hold the truth. Relay carries the argument.

Durable Actors (Terse)

  • One actor per component, plant and company
  • State, history, calibration and rules live in the actor
  • One call at a time per actor, so decisions stay in order
  • A setpoint needs a one-time token from the execute path

Agent Relay

  • Ten component agents on plant and company channels
  • Proposals, votes and counters travel as messages
  • Cross-plant votes go over the company channel
  • Results reach the agents, which score their predictions
